Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Rental Houses Kenya is operated by Referraliq Solutions in Kenya. This policy explains what information we collect through the Rental Houses Kenya website and account services, why we collect it, how we use and share it, how long we keep it, and how you can make a privacy request. It applies to visitors and to landlord, tenant, caretaker and administrator accounts.

1. Information we collect and where it comes from

We collect information that you provide, information created when you use platform features, and limited information returned by authentication, payment and communication providers.

  • Account and identity information: name, email address, phone number, account role and status, password authentication data handled by Supabase Auth, email and phone verification status, and authenticator-factor status for administrator accounts.
  • Google sign-in information: if you choose “Continue with Google,” Google returns a stable account identifier and basic profile information such as your name and email address. The app requests these basic identity details for authentication; it does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Contacts, Drive files, Calendar, or other Google content.
  • Landlord and property information: landlord profile and verification details; property title, county, town or area, house type, rent and deposit, unit counts, amenities, rules, electricity notes and listing photographs.
  • Tenant and caretaker information: names, phone numbers, tenancy and unit details, rent and deposit terms, due dates, balances, invitation status, caretaker assignments and information needed to provide account features.
  • Viewing and communications information: a prospective tenant’s name and phone number, selected property, requested viewing date and time, verification-code status, viewing status and notes, messages, complaints, notices, SMS text, recipients, segment counts and delivery status.
  • Landlord verification documents: national identity and KRA information, and uploaded identity-document images and KRA documents submitted for review.
  • Payment and service records: M-Pesa payment attempts, amounts, transaction and receipt references, rent ledger and payout records, subscription payments, SMS-wallet balances and top-ups, and relevant provider identifiers. We do not request or store your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Security and technical information: authentication sessions, account and security events, timestamps, and hashed network identifiers used to limit verification-code abuse. We do not use this service to build advertising profiles or track users across unrelated websites.

2. How we access and use Google user data

When you choose Google sign-in, the sign-in flow uses Google OAuth through Supabase Auth. The application receives the Google account identifier and basic profile details returned for the requested identity scopes, principally your name and email address. We use these details to authenticate you, create or identify your Rental Houses Kenya landlord account, display your account identity, maintain your profile, and protect sign-in. Google sign-in creates or accesses a landlord account; it does not bypass landlord verification, administrator approval or subscription requirements.

The Google account identifier and authentication identity are processed by Supabase Auth. The email address and name used by the application are stored in the platform’s account/profile records. Authentication session tokens are maintained by the browser authentication client so that you remain signed in. Google authentication responses are handled by Google and Supabase as part of completing sign-in.

We share the sign-in request and the information needed to authenticate your account with Google and Supabase. We do not sell Google user data, share it with advertisers, use it for targeted advertising or creditworthiness decisions, or use it to train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models. We use Google user data only to provide, maintain and secure Google sign-in and the associated account, to meet legal obligations, or to respond to your request.

You can stop using Google sign-in and may remove the connection in your Google Account security settings. Revoking the connection may prevent future Google sign-in; it does not itself delete your Rental Houses Kenya account or information already held by the platform. Contact us to request deletion of platform account information.

3. How we use other information

We use account details to create and secure accounts, authenticate users, assign account roles and communicate about account status. Landlord and property information is used to review landlord eligibility, publish and manage listings, calculate plan charges and administer properties. Tenant, caretaker and viewing information is used to manage invitations, tenancies, viewing requests, notices, complaints, rent balances and account access.

Payment information is used to initiate or reconcile M-Pesa payments, maintain transaction and payout records, operate subscriptions and SMS wallets, prevent duplicate or fraudulent transactions and answer payment questions. Phone numbers and message text are used to send the verification code, receipt, reminder or other SMS requested by the applicable feature and to record its status. Security information is used to protect accounts, investigate misuse and enforce rate limits.

We process information when needed to provide the service or take steps you request, to meet legal obligations, for legitimate interests such as account security and fraud prevention, and on consent where required. You may withdraw consent for future processing where consent is the basis.

4. Public listings and information access

Vacant property listings are public and may show the property title, county, town or area, house type, rent, deposit, amenities, availability and listing photographs. Public listings are designed not to show the landlord’s phone number, email address or exact property location. When you request a viewing, the name, verified phone number, selected property and preferred date and time are made available to the relevant landlord so they can respond and arrange the viewing.

Private account information is restricted according to account role and database access controls. Landlords can access their own property and tenancy information; tenants can access information relating to their own tenancy; caretakers receive limited information for assigned properties; and administrators may access information needed for verification, security, support, payment reconciliation and platform administration. Identity and KRA files are stored separately in private storage for authorized review.

5. When we share information

  • Google: receives the OAuth sign-in request and processes authentication when you choose Google sign-in.
  • Supabase: hosts authentication, the database, private file storage and server functions. It processes account credentials and sessions, profile and service records, uploaded files and information needed to run requested features.
  • Render: hosts and serves the public website and its static assets.
  • Safaricom: processes M-Pesa requests and callbacks. Payment details needed to initiate, confirm or reconcile a transaction are sent to or received from Safaricom.
  • Celcom: receives a destination phone number and message text when the platform sends an SMS and may return a message identifier or delivery response.
  • Other users: landlords receive viewing requests and information needed to manage their own properties and tenancies. Tenants and caretakers receive information displayed in their authorized account areas.

We may disclose limited information if required by law or valid legal process, to protect users or the service, or to investigate fraud, abuse or security incidents. We do not sell personal information or permit providers to use it for their own advertising. Providers may process information in other countries under their terms and applicable safeguards.

6. Storage, security and retention

Information is stored in the platform’s Supabase project and in provider systems that support authentication, hosting, SMS and payment processing. House photographs are resized and stripped of embedded location metadata before upload. Identity documents are held in private storage. Saved landlord Daraja credentials are encrypted before storage.

We use role-based access controls, private document storage, encrypted payment credentials, authentication controls and security logging. These measures reduce risk but cannot guarantee absolute security. Protect your password and devices, sign out on shared devices, and contact us promptly if you suspect account compromise.

We keep account and profile information while an account is active and as needed to provide its features. Listings and related photographs are retained while published or needed to administer the landlord’s account. Viewing, tenancy, message and complaint information is kept while needed to provide the requested service, handle follow-up and maintain related records. Identity and KRA documents are kept for landlord verification, fraud prevention, legal compliance and dispute handling, then securely removed when no longer needed, subject to legal holds.

Payment, subscription, payout, audit and security records may be retained after an account closes for accounting, legal compliance, fraud prevention or dispute resolution. Some financial and audit records are append-only in the current system and cannot be erased through the user interface. We do not currently offer an automatic account-deletion button or publish fixed deletion periods by record type. We review deletion requests individually and remove or de-identify information where it is no longer needed and applicable law and system records permit. Provider backups and records may persist under the provider’s retention cycle.

7. Your choices and privacy requests

Subject to applicable law, you may ask whether we process your information, request access or correction, ask how it is used, object to or request restriction of certain processing, request a copy of information you provided, request deletion where applicable, or withdraw consent where consent is the basis. We may need to verify your identity. Requests may be limited where information must be kept for legal, financial-record, safety or dispute-resolution purposes.

To request access, correction, export or deletion, email siskemejja@gmail.com from the email associated with your account if possible. Use the subject “Privacy request,” describe what you are requesting, and include the account email. We will handle the request within the period required by applicable law. Do not email passwords, M-Pesa PINs or identity-document attachments unless we provide a secure method. You may also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner in Kenya.

8. Children, changes and contact

The service is intended for adults arranging or managing rental housing, not for children to use independently. If you believe a child has submitted personal information, contact us so we can review it.

We will publish the current policy on this page and update its effective date when it changes. We will provide additional notice where material changes or applicable law require it. If our use of Google user data changes, we will update this policy before using that data for a new purpose.
